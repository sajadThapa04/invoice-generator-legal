Support

We’re here if something feels stuck.

Invoice Generator is a local app — most fixes live in Settings. For billing and refunds, Apple is the right place.

Restore Pro

Open Settings in the app, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID that bought Pro. If nothing appears, check your subscription under iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, or from Manage subscription inside the app. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us.

Backups

Export a backup from Settings if you want a copy of your invoices and settings. Restoring a backup replaces what is currently on the device — take care before confirming.

Camera and scanning

Smart Capture needs Camera or Photos permission. If scans fail, check Privacy & Security settings for Invoice Generator, then try again in good light.
