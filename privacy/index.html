Privacy Policy

Your business stays on your phone.

Invoice Generator is built so invoices, clients, rates, logos, and scans do not go to our servers. We don’t run an account system, and we don’t sell your data.

Effective 14 September 2026

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Invoice Generator iOS app published by Sajad Kumar Thapa (“we”, “us”).

What we store on your device

The app keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad, using on-device storage (including UserDefaults for settings):

  • Business profile, logo, default currency, tax, and payment notes
  • Clients, saved rates, invoices, and recently deleted invoices
  • PDF templates you choose, and whether Pro is unlocked
  • Optional backups you export as a file on the device

That information never leaves the device unless you share it yourself — for example by sending a PDF, exporting a backup, or using iOS share sheets.

What we do not collect

  • We do not create an account for you
  • We do not operate our own servers for invoice data
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • We do not sell or rent personal information

Camera and photos

If you allow access, the camera and photo library are used only to:

  • Scan a business card or a list of what you charge (Smart Capture)
  • Choose a business logo for invoice PDFs

Photos and scans are processed on the device to fill in fields. We do not upload them to our servers.

Smart Capture and Apple Intelligence

Text recognition uses Apple’s on-device Vision framework. On supported devices, optional Apple Intelligence features may tidy scanned text into client or line-item fields. We do not send that text to our own servers. Apple’s system privacy rules apply to Apple Intelligence.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions are sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payment, Apple ID, and restore-purchase data. We do not see your full payment details. See also Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is meant for business invoicing, not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub, or use the contact details on the App Store listing for Invoice Generator.
